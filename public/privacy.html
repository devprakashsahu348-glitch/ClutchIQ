Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Who we are

ClutchIQ is operated by Dev Prakash Sahu, India. Contact: devprakashsahu348@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account data: name, email address and a password hash (we never store your password).
  • Contact messages: name, email and message text.
  • Campaign data: UTM parameters from the link you arrived through, saved with your account.
  • Technical data: IP address, used briefly for rate limiting and security.
  • Analytics: anonymised usage data, only if you accept analytics cookies.

How we use it

To run your account, secure the service, answer messages, send password-reset emails and improve the product. We do not sell personal data.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in a Neon PostgreSQL database. The site is hosted on Vercel. Emails are sent through our email provider. These providers process data on our behalf.

Retention and your rights

We keep account data until you ask us to delete it. You may request access, correction or deletion at any time by emailing devprakashsahu348@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days, in line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Children

ClutchIQ is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when this policy changes.
