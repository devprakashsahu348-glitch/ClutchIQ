Methodology

Last updated 16 November 2026

1. Pressure index

For every delivery we compute a score from 0 to 1 from the state of the innings before the ball.

  • Chasing: 0.55 × required-rate stress (required rate above 7.5, capped at 13.5) + 0.25 × wickets lost ÷ 10 + 0.20 × share of the innings gone.
  • Batting first: 0.6 × (half wickets lost + half share of innings gone), because there is no target to chase.
  • Knockout matches add 0.1.

A delivery counts as a pressure situation when its score is in the top 20 percent (0.452 or higher). That gives 40,364 pressure deliveries out of 200,961.

2. Does it separate pressure from ordinary play?

Ordinary1.404.8%160,597

Wickets fall about 64 percent more often under pressure, so the index picks out genuinely harder moments.

3. Performance measure

Each ball gets an impact value against the league average for the same format (IPL-style league or T20I), innings and phase (powerplay, middle, death), calculated separately for pressure and ordinary balls. A wicket is valued at 8 runs.

  • Batters: (runs − expected runs) − 8 × (dismissed − expected dismissal rate). Wides are excluded.
  • Bowlers: (expected runs conceded − runs conceded, including wides and no-balls) + 8 × (wicket − expected wicket rate). Run-outs are not credited.

4. Rating and fairness

  • Eligibility: at least 120 pressure balls and 15 matches with 6 or more pressure balls. 27 batters and 71 bowlers qualify.
  • Shrinkage: impact per 100 balls is multiplied by n ÷ (n + 60) so short spells cannot dominate.
  • Rating = 0.60 × z(shrunk impact) + 0.25 × z(consistency, the share of matches with positive impact) + 0.15 × z(lift over the player’s own non-pressure baseline).
  • IPL and T20I are pooled, but expectations are set within each format so neither inflates the other.

5. Limitations

Strict eligibility leaves a modest batter pool. Weights, the 80th-percentile cut-off and the wicket value are judgement calls and are fixed in analysis/pressure_model.py. Opposition quality, venue and batting order are not adjusted for. Super overs are excluded.
