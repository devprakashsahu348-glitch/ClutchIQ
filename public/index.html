Frequently asked questions

What counts as pressure?

Every delivery gets a pressure score from 0 to 1 using the required run rate, wickets lost, how late in the innings it is and whether the match is a knockout. The top 20 percent of deliveries (score of 0.452 or more) are treated as pressure situations.

Which data is used?

867 IPL and T20I matches from 2022 onwards, covering 200,961 deliveries.

How are players compared fairly?

Each ball is compared with the league average for the same format, innings and phase, so openers and finishers are not judged against the same yardstick. Small samples are shrunk towards zero.

Do I need an account?

The top three in each list are public. Create a free account to see the full top 25 batters and bowlers.

Can I copy the rankings?

Yes. The dashboard has a copy button for each top 10 list.
